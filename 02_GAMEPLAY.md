# Gameplay Design

## Gameplay Loop

1.  Hatch Egg
2.  Feed, clean, play
3.  Train using mini-games
4.  Explore/events
5.  Battle nearby (Bluetooth) or online
6.  Evolve
7.  Build Legacy
8.  Repeat

## Monster Lifecycle

Egg -\> Infant -\> Child -\> Teen -\> Adult -\> Elder -\> Death

Death grants Legacy Data which unlocks cosmetics, encyclopedia entries
and future eggs.

## Core Stats

Health, Energy, Attack, Defense, Speed, Intelligence, Spirit,
Friendship, Hunger, Mood, Cleanliness, Fatigue, Age, Weight.

## Hidden Values

Genetics, Personality, Care Mistakes, Battle History.

## Personality

Aggressive, Calm, Curious, Lazy, Loyal, Brave, Cowardly, Playful.

## Evolution

Determined by combinations of: - Care quality - Wins/losses - Training
focus - Personality - Genetics - Age - Special items - Time-based events

## Battles

Preparation matters more than player input. Bluetooth exchanges only
battle state and random seed. Battle duration: 20-45 seconds.

## Multiplayer

Bluetooth LE: - Nearby battle - Trading - Co-op events

Online: - Ranked - Friends - Guilds - Seasonal events

## Monetization

Cosmetics only. No pay-to-win.
